Hands off the hedges

Help document hedges and landscaping in our neighborhood. It takes two steps and about a minute.

1

Take the photo with your regular Camera app

Get the whole hedge in the frame, plus the house number or street sign if you can. Your phone saves the location in the photo automatically.

2

Upload it

Tap the button below, then Choose files → Photo Library. You can pick several photos at once. Type your name and email, then tap Upload. No Dropbox account needed.

Upload photos →
Make sure your camera saves location

This is usually on already. To check:

  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Camera → While Using the App, with Precise Location on.
  • Android: open the Camera app → Settings (gear) → turn on Location tags or Save location.
Tips
  • Pick photos from your Photo Library. Taking a new photo from inside the upload page may not keep the location.
  • Stay on public sidewalks and streets. Don't enter anyone's property.
  • Daylight photos work best.

Photos go to the Hands Off the Hedges organizer only. Each photo includes the location where it was taken.
